Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

The short version

  • We collect only what you send us through our forms or messages, plus basic server logs.
  • We use it to reply to you, review Academy applications, and keep the site secure.
  • We never sell your data, and this site sets no advertising or analytics cookies.
  • You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your data at any time.

1. Who we are

Data and Design Geeks Studio (“DDGs”, “we”, “us”) is a digital studio based in Abuja, Nigeria, offering product design, web and mobile development, data analytics, cybersecurity, and training. We are the controller of the personal data collected through this website.

Questions about this policy or your data: dataanddesigngeeks@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Project inquiry form: your name, email address, the service you are interested in, and the project brief you write.
  • Academy application form: your name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number (optional), country and city, the track, cohort and learning mode you choose, your experience level and weekly availability, your written answers about your goals and background, an optional portfolio or profile link, and whether you want to be considered for a scholarship. You also confirm that you are 18 or older.
  • Direct messages: anything you send us by email or WhatsApp, including your contact details.

Information collected automatically

  • Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested, date and time). We use these only to keep the site secure and working.

Information stored on your device

  • Your light or dark theme choice is saved in your browser’s local storage under the name ddg-theme. It stays on your device and is never sent to us.

We do not ask for sensitive information (such as health data or government ID numbers). Please do not include it in your project brief or Academy application.

3. How we use your data and why

  • To reply to your inquiry and prepare a proposal. Basis: taking steps at your request before a contract, and our legitimate interest in running our business.
  • To review your Academy application, contact you about a call, and tell you the decision. Basis: taking steps at your request before a contract. If you ask us to tell you about future cohorts, we do so based on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • To keep the site secure and prevent spam or abuse. Basis: our legitimate interest.
  • To meet legal and accounting obligations. Basis: legal obligation.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for automated decision-making or advertising profiles.

4. Who we share it with

We share data only with service providers that help us run the site and respond to you, and only as needed:

  • Form processing and email providers that deliver your form submission to our inbox.
  • Website hosting and content delivery providers that serve this site.
  • Google Fonts, which delivers the fonts used on this site. Your browser requests them directly from Google, so Google receives your IP address.
  • Unsplash, from which some sample images are loaded. Your browser requests them directly, so Unsplash receives your IP address.
  • Professional advisers (such as lawyers or accountants) and authorities, where the law requires it.

These providers act on our instructions or under their own privacy terms. We may update this list as the site changes.

5. International transfers

We work from Nigeria and with clients worldwide, and some of our providers process data in other countries, including the United States and the European Union. Where we transfer personal data across borders, we do so in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and, where it applies, the GDPR, using appropriate safeguards such as contractual protections.

6. How long we keep it

  • Project inquiries: up to 24 months after our last contact, unless we start a project together. Project records are then kept as our contract and legal obligations require.
  • Academy applications: up to 12 months after the decision on your application. If you enrol, we keep your records as our enrolment terms and legal obligations require. You can ask us to delete your application earlier.
  • Server logs: a short period set by our hosting provider, usually no more than 90 days.

When the retention period ends, we delete or anonymise the data.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct data that is wrong or incomplete;
  • delete your data;
  • limit or stop how we use it;
  • give you your data in a portable format;
  • stop using it for any purpose based on our legitimate interest, or withdraw consent you gave us.

To use any of these rights, email dataanddesigngeeks@gmail.com. We reply within one month. We may need to confirm your identity first.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) or, if you live elsewhere, to your local data protection authority. We would appreciate the chance to fix the problem first.

8. How we protect your data

This site is served over HTTPS. We limit access to your submissions to the people who need them, and we choose providers that use industry-standard security. No system is completely secure, so please avoid sending confidential material through the contact form. Ask us for a secure channel first.

9. Children

Our services and this website are for adults and organisations. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you think a child has sent us data, email us and we will delete it.

10. Cookies and similar technologies

This site does not set advertising or analytics cookies. The only item stored on your device is your theme preference, described above. If we add analytics or other tools in future, we will update this page and ask for consent where the law requires it.

11. Links to other sites

This site links to services such as Behance, GitHub, Dribbble, Facebook, and WhatsApp. They have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for how they handle your data.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “last updated” date at the top shows when it last changed. If we make a significant change, we will say so on the home page.
