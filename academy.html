What you need

  • A laptop that can run a web browser and a code editor
  • A reliable internet connection
  • 10 to 12 hours a week for 12 weeks
  • Age 16 or older
  • Curiosity and the habit of finishing what you start

Is it for you?

Career changers, graduates, self-taught builders, and professionals who want a new skill all join us. You do not need a degree. If you are willing to practise every week and show your work, you will fit in.

How applying works

We use a short application and a friendly call instead of an entrance exam, because motivation and consistency predict success better than a test score.

  1. 01

    Apply

    Fill in the 4-step form. It takes about 10 minutes, and you do not need a CV.

  2. 02

    We review

    A real person reads every application and replies within 5 working days.

  3. 03

    Short call

    Shortlisted applicants have a friendly 20-minute chat about goals and fit. No exam, no trick questions.

  4. 04

    Decision and onboarding

    You hear within 3 working days. Accepted students get fees, schedule, and a welcome pack.
