Digital Ajo Savings App

A new brand and app design that makes group savings (ajo and esusu) feel modern, clear and trustworthy.

Nigerian savings startup (name withheld)

Fintech

The challenge

The startup lets friends, families and colleagues run rotating savings groups, known as ajo or esusu, from their phones. Early users liked the idea but did not trust the app with their money, and the look felt generic.

Users ranged from first-time smartphone owners to salaried professionals. The design had to feel friendly, be easy to read, and show clearly where each naira is at every step.

Our approach

  1. 01

    Research

    Spoke with 16 people who save in groups, from traders in Mile 12 to office workers in Abuja.

  2. 02

    Identity

    Developed a warm, confident visual identity with a simple logo, colour palette and tone of voice.

  3. 03

    Interface

    Designed the full app flow, including creating a group, contributions and payouts.

  4. 04

    Handoff

    Prepared a design system and developer notes, and reviewed the first builds.

The solution

A new brand and a clear, friendly interface where every group shows who has paid, who is next, and when money moves. Trust cues such as verified members, receipts and plain-language rules appear at each step.

  • Group timeline showing contributions and payout order
  • Plain-language rules and receipts for every transaction
  • Large text, strong contrast and a low-data design
  • Copy prepared in English, Pidgin, Yoruba and Hausa

The outcome

After the redesign, more first-time visitors finished sign-up and created or joined a group. Support questions about where a payment had gone fell, and the founders used the new identity in their investor and partner conversations.
