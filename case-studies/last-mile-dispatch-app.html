Last-Mile Dispatch App

A rider app and dispatch console that shortened delivery times for a company serving Lagos and Abuja.

Client
Lagos and Abuja delivery company (name withheld)
Industry
Logistics
Location
Lagos and Abuja
Timeline
16 weeks, 2025
Our role
Product design, mobile and backend engineering
Platform
Android rider app and web dispatch console

Key results

1,800

daily deliveries managed at peak

22%

shorter average delivery time

35%

fewer failed or re-attempted deliveries

The challenge

Dispatchers assigned orders by phone and WhatsApp, so riders took long routes, customers called to ask where their parcel was, and managers had no clear picture of the day.

Traffic in Lagos, unclear addresses and unreliable mobile data made tracking and routing hard. Riders use low-cost phones and pay for their own data, so the app had to be light.

Our approach

  1. 01

    Discover

    Rode along with riders in Lagos and Abuja and mapped how an order moves from request to doorstep.

  2. 02

    Design

    Designed a one-hand rider app and a dispatcher console built around a live map.

  3. 03

    Build

    Built both apps and a matching and routing service, and tested them with 25 riders.

  4. 04

    Launch

    Rolled out zone by zone, with rider training days and daily support for two weeks.

The solution

A lightweight Android app for riders and a web console for dispatchers. Orders are matched to the nearest available rider, routes are suggested using live traffic, and customers get tracking links by SMS.

  • Automatic matching of orders to nearby riders
  • Route suggestions using Google Maps Platform
  • Proof of delivery with a photo and customer code
  • Tracking links sent by SMS and WhatsApp

The outcome

Deliveries became faster and more predictable. Dispatchers manage more orders each, customers call less to ask for updates, and managers see daily performance by rider and by zone.
