Microfinance Mobile Banking App

A security-hardened SME financial ledger and real-time cash-flow forecasting platform powered by Google Gemini AI and OPay API integration.

The challenge

Between 80% and 95% of Nigerian SMEs fail within five years due to poor record-keeping and cash-flow mismanagement, leaving informal operators flying blind without visibility into burn rate or runway.

Conventional accounting tools are too complex for informal traders, while lightweight ledger alternatives completely ignore cybersecurity, exposing sensitive transaction data, revenues, and supplier costs to fraud, SQL injection, and identity theft.

Our approach

  1. 01

    Discover

    Analyzed bookkeeping friction and cyber threats faced by Nigerian informal retailers and open-air market distributors.

  2. 02

    Design

    Designed a high-fidelity four-screen prototype in Figma featuring a Zero-Trust gateway and plain-language ledger.

  3. 03

    Build

    Integrated Google Gemini API into the backend analytical core as a Virtual CFO alongside server-level SQLi filtering and rate limiting.

  4. 04

    Launch

    Conducted penetration testing, NDPR compliance auditing, and simulated live transaction integration with the OPay Business API.

The solution

A security-first financial ecosystem that pairs plain-language bookkeeping with Google Gemini AI for automated cash-flow forecasting and insolvency alerts, wrapped in an enterprise-grade defense architecture featuring 2FA, cryptographic PII masking, and live threat logging.

  • Zero-Trust Gateway with 2FA, biometric authentication prompts, and compliance badges
  • Financial Ledger Dashboard tracking gross income, expenses, and net operational cash position in plain language
  • Gemini AI Forecasting Hub featuring a Risk Score Dial and Insolvency Runway Countdown
  • Live Security Compliance Hub displaying real-time terminal logs for SQLi sanitization, rate limiting, and NDPR audits

The outcome

Delivered an intuitive bookkeeping experience that eliminates accounting jargon for informal traders while generating verified transaction audit trails for formal credit scoring, backed by a resilient security core that actively neutralizes perimeter attacks.
