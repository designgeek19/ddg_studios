Payments Operations Dashboard

A real-time dashboard that helps a Lagos payments team reconcile transactions and catch failed settlements in minutes.

The challenge

The client processes card, transfer and USSD payments for merchants across Lagos and Abuja. Transaction data lived in separate bank portals, gateway exports and spreadsheets, so the finance team spent most mornings matching records by hand.

Failed and delayed settlements were often found by merchants before the team noticed. The company needed one screen it could trust, built with strict access control and the reporting that CBN-regulated businesses are expected to keep.

Our approach

  1. 01

    Discover

    Shadowed the finance and support teams for a week and mapped every report they used.

  2. 02

    Design

    Prototyped three dashboard layouts and tested them with six finance officers.

  3. 03

    Build

    Built the web app and API layer in two-week sprints, with a demo every Friday.

  4. 04

    Launch

    Ran the old and new process side by side for a month, then trained all three teams.

The solution

We replaced the scattered exports with a single web dashboard fed by gateway and bank settlement files. Reconciliation runs automatically, mismatches are flagged by amount and age, and each person sees only what their role allows.

  • Automatic matching of gateway, bank and ledger records
  • Alerts for failed, delayed and duplicate settlements
  • Role-based access with a full audit trail
  • One-click exports for finance and compliance reports

The outcome

Within the first quarter, daily reconciliation dropped from most of a morning to under an hour. Support agents now answer merchant questions from the dashboard instead of waiting for the finance team, and settlement problems are usually caught before a merchant calls.
